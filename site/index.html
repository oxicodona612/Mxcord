Mxcord

Your Matrix direct messages, inside Discord for Android.

Install

  1. In Discord, open Settings › Revenge › Plugins and tap +.
  2. Paste the plugin URL above and confirm the warning.
  3. Open the Mxcord settings, enter your homeserver, username and password, and tap Log in.

Your Matrix DMs show up in the DM list right away. Revenge checks for updates each time Discord starts.

Features

Native DMs

Matrix conversations sit in the DM list next to your Discord chats, sorted by activity, with previews.

Full messaging

Send, reply, edit and delete. Older history loads as you scroll.

End-to-end encryption

Encrypted rooms work through pantalaimon running next to your homeserver.

Profiles

Avatars and display names come from Matrix and stay up to date.

Message requests

DM invites appear under Message Requests and can be accepted or ignored.

Notifications

New messages notify like any other DM. Start new chats with Add by Matrix.

Privacy

Mxcord runs entirely on your phone and only talks to the homeserver you choose. Messages go over Matrix and never touch Discord's servers. Any Discord request that refers to a Matrix chat is answered on the device instead of being sent.

Requirements

  • Discord for Android with Revenge or another Vendetta-compatible mod.
  • A Matrix account on a homeserver reachable over HTTPS.
  • pantalaimon for encrypted rooms.
